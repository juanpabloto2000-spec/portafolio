# Business Triage Skill - Dynamind Studios

This skill enables an autonomous agent to evaluate a client business model, identify fatal operational leaks (overbooking, manual WhatsApp chat friction, blind cash register discrepancies), and output a prioritized architectural implementation plan.

## Capabilities
- Detect whether a business suffers from D0 bottleneck (manual order taking, double booking, stock shrinkage).
- Recommend whether a custom Operational Core (PMS/KDS/ERP) or an n8n WhatsApp agent swarm is required.
- Estimate immediate free hours gained for the business founder.
